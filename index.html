The Studio

Honest storytelling.
Nothing staged.

OUT OF FRAME is an independent wedding film and post-production studio built around honest storytelling.

We capture the moments that happen naturally, the people who make the day, and the feeling you can't recreate. Through intentional cinematography, thoughtful editing, and a raw, cinematic approach, we turn real life into films worth coming back to.

No forced moments. No unnecessary production. Just your story, told honestly.

The Meaning Behind The Name

The moments that define a relationship aren't always the ones in front of the camera. They're the quiet glances, the shaky laughter, the unplanned speeches, the people in the background, and the moments that happen between the big moments.

That's the philosophy behind OUT OF FRAME: capturing the things you can't stage.

Questions

Good to know.

How do I reserve my date?

Send an inquiry or call us, then place a deposit using the "Pay Deposit & Book" button. Your date is held as soon as the deposit is received.

What's your filming style?

Documentary-driven and low-profile. We capture moments as they happen — natural audio, real emotion, no forced poses.

Do you travel outside Springfield?

Yes. We're based in Springfield, TN and happily travel for weddings and events. Reach out with your location for details.

I'm a filmmaker — can you edit my footage?

Absolutely. Our creative partnership service offers editing, color grading, sound design and finishing while preserving your own style.

When will I receive my film?

Timelines depend on the scope of your project. We'll outline a clear delivery schedule when you book.
