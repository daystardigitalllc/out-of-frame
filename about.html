About

Hi, I'm Dan.

I'm a storyteller. I capture real moments and turn them into films that feel authentic, cinematic and timeless.

I'm originally from Michigan, where I graduated from Specs Howard School of Media Arts in 2012 with a degree in Post Production.

I started out filming wedding videos and cinematic special events: sporting events, retirement parties and ceremonies, graduation parties, baby showers, engagement parties and more.

Today I'm married and a dad to two wild boys, and I still believe some stories deserve to be told well.

Behind the camera too

I edit every film myself.

My work doesn't stop at the camera. I handle post-production too: editing, color grading, sound design and the finishing touches that bring a story to life.

The best films are built on genuine emotion, intentional storytelling and a raw, cinematic style that feels real rather than overproduced.

Out of Frame is how I do that for you, so you and your family have something to keep for a lifetime.
